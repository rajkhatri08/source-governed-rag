Data Retention Policy
Version 1.0

Section 1. Retention Periods
Customer transaction records shall be retained for a period of three years from the date of the transaction, after which they must be securely deleted.

Section 2. Breach Notification
In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Protection Officer must be notified within 30 days of discovery. The DPO will assess whether external notification is required.

Section 3. Access Requests
Requests from data subjects for access to their personal data will be processed within 60 days of receipt.
