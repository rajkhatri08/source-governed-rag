Draft AI Usage Policy
Version 0.3 - DRAFT, NOT APPROVED

Section 1. Scope
This policy governs the use of artificial intelligence systems that process personal data.

Section 2. Data Subject Requests
Requests relating to automated decision-making must be answered within 14 days of receipt.

Section 3. Breach Notification
Any breach involving an AI system must be reported to the supervisory authority within 24 hours of discovery.
